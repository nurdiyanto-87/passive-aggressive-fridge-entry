# Candidate lines — judged against the brief (most important first)

Criteria: (1) genuinely a fridge note; (2) real joke, turn/sting unseen;
(3) fridge voice — smug about sensors/inventory/door alarms, petty about
habits; (4) small domestic grudge, not a rant/threat; (5) fresh concrete
detail (yogurt, ice maker, leftovers) over generic nagging. No explaining
the joke; trust the reader.

## A (107 chars)
"Your yogurt expired Tuesday. I waited until Friday to tell you, because I wanted you to feel it first."
- Hits 1, 3, 4, 5 (yogurt, withholding info = petty). Weakness: "because I
  wanted you to feel it first" half-explains the joke; two clauses dilute
  the sting. Good but not best.

## B (76 chars)
"Door alarm #47 today. At this point I'm not warning you, I'm keeping score."
- Hits 1, 3 (door alarm count = sensor smugness), 4. Turn "keeping score"
  is punchy and short. Weakness: no concrete food detail (criterion 5);
  "door alarm" alone is the most generic fridge complaint in the field.

## C (91 chars)
"The ice maker and I have agreed: you only deserve cubes when you stop leaving the door open."
- Hits 1, 3, 5 (ice maker). "Have agreed" is a fun anthropomorphic turn.
  Weakness: "only deserve cubes" is a mild sting and the line reads like a
  rule, not a note left on the door.

## D (75 chars) — WINNER
"I watched you put the milk back warm again. The yogurt saw it too. We talk."
- Hits all five: fridge note (1); three-beat escalation with the sting in
  the last two words (2); "I watched you" = sensor smugness (3); warm milk
  put back is a tiny domestic grudge (4); milk + yogurt are concrete (5).
  Nothing explains the joke — the reader has to picture the fridge
  gossiping with the yogurt. Shortest of the strong candidates (tie-break
  favours fewer bytes).

## E (89 chars)
"I kept your leftovers at a perfect 3°C for six days. They have now filed for a new home."
- Hits 1, 3, 5. Weakness: "filed for a new home" is absurd rather than
  funny — the turn lands oddly, and the grudge reads as a complaint letter.

## F (103 chars)
"You opened me 23 times looking for a snack and closed me empty-handed 23 times. I am not a museum."
- Hits 1, 3, 4. Weakness: "I am not a museum" is a worn trope; the sting
  is predictable, so criterion 2 is the weakest of the six.

## Verdict
D wins: strongest turn ("We talk"), freshest concrete detail (yogurt as a
witness), shortest, and the most fridge-voiced. A is the runner-up.
